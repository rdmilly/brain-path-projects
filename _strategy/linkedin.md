# LinkedIn Posting Strategy

**Brand:** Millyweb Development + RevenueFirst.AI  
**Tone:** Professional, insightful, lessons-learned

---

## Content Guidelines

### DO:
- Lead with insight or lesson learned
- Include specific numbers/metrics when available
- End with forward-looking statement ("Next up...")
- Use relevant hashtags (#BuildingInPublic #AI #Automation)
- Keep paragraphs short (2-3 lines max)

### DON'T:
- Use excessive emojis
- Sound too casual or meme-y
- Include raw error messages
- Share unverified claims

---

## Post Structure

```
[Hook - What happened or what you learned]

[Context - Brief background]

[Insight - The key takeaway]

[Next - What's coming]

#Hashtags
```

---

## Example

```
Deployed Mautic today as part of my open-source marketing stack.

Key learning: Don't underestimate memory requirements. 
Mautic needs 2GB RAM minimum for email campaigns.

Next up: Connecting it to my CRM.

#BuildingInPublic #MarTech #OpenSource
```

---

## Posting Schedule

- **Time:** 6:00 PM PT (optimal for engagement)
- **Frequency:** 1 post per day (from project rotation)
- **Sunday:** Weekly recap of all projects
